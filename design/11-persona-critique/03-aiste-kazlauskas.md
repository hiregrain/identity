# Aiste Kazlauskas, 38, Vilnius

Independent industrial designer, eleven years freelance, clients across
Europe and two in the US. Roughly forty clients: three-week jobs and
two-year retainers, several overlapping, four through agencies where the
end client never knew her name, two under NDA, three companies that no
longer exist, one relationship that ended in a payment dispute and a
lawyer. A portfolio site does most of her selling and most work comes by
referral.

Her practical problem is that her work history is not a list, it is a
mesh. Every system built for four employers in a row makes her look
either unemployed or scattered. She also has a real trust problem with
any platform holding her client list, because her client list is her
business.

Evidence tier: synthetic. Binds nothing. See [`README.md`](README.md).

## Walkthrough

**Consent.** "A chapter is one job or engagement with one business." One
business. My eleven years is roughly forty engagements with forty
businesses, four of which were agency work where the end client never
knew my name, so I am already doing arithmetic about whether this expects
forty separate items.

Then, still above the fold: "There is no way to show them part of it."
That is the whole product, decided, on the signup screen. My client list
is my business. This says the only way to prove one engagement is to hand
over all forty, including the two under NDA and the one that ended with a
lawyer. If a prospective client in Munich asks me for a record, I show
them everything, or nothing.

I do respect that they told me up front rather than in a settings screen
two weeks later. Nobody writes a consent screen this honest unless they
mean it. Honest about a design that is wrong for me is still wrong for
me. I tap agree once, out of curiosity, but I have already decided I am
evaluating this, not adopting it.

**Handle.** Fine, and the permanence guarantee is the right guarantee
stated plainly. One question it does not answer: my selling is done by my
portfolio site, so what is this URL for. It reads like it expects to be
my primary address and it will not be.

**Empty.** "Add where you have worked" is employee language. I did not
work at anywhere. I worked for people, from my own desk, often three at
once. Both doors on this screen are doors for someone with a job history:
I have not had a resume in eleven years.

"Work nobody has confirmed is drawn plain" tells me exactly what my
record will look like. Three clients no longer exist. Four were agencies.
Two are NDA. That is nine chapters permanently plain before I ask anyone.

**Imported.** "None of it counts until it is" lands hardest. I would
arrive here with forty rows, all plain, and a screen telling me none of
it counts, then a button that per the consent screen is irreversible.

The list shape has an "Engagement" type, which is at least a word for
what I do. But two of the five sample rows overlap by two years and the
layout says nothing about it. At five rows that reads as untidy dates. At
my forty, with six live at once in a busy spring, it reads as a person
who cannot keep her story straight. I would tap back before committing.
This is the screen where I want a draft state and there is not one.

**Main.** The ring is beautiful and I will say plainly, as someone who
does this for a living, that the drawing is good work. It is also, for
the first several sessions, the largest object on my home screen carrying
information I cannot read. I do not know what one lobe means. Nobody I
send this to will know either.

The list underneath is honest in a way I like. Dates confirmed but not
the work is a real distinction and I have never seen a system draw it.

Sunrise Foods nests two roles inside one business. Good. But that is
sequential nesting inside one business, and my problem is the opposite:
six businesses in one season. There is no construct for that.

The footer is the payment dispute problem in one sentence. If I ask that
client to confirm and they write something sour, it is permanent and my
only remedy is destroying eleven years.

**Expanded.** The screen that made me take it seriously and then stop.
The one-line explanation of the figure is a real explanation and it
arrives immediately.

Now look at what the seven do to me. I direct nobody. The systems are the
client's factory. I quote a fee and deliver. The client signs off. Four
of seven lobes are structurally flat for every independent contractor
alive, and the figure will be visibly dented on the same four sides
forever. A client reads lopsided as small. Nothing on this screen says
these measures describe a role inside an organisation.

Then: they have not been tested for whether two managers pick the same
one. I would rather know than not know. But it means the thing occupying
the top half of my home screen is admittedly uncalibrated, and it is the
thing that travels.

Seven copies of the same paragraph is a wall. Say it once at the top.

**Landing.** Well made and I do not need it. What I want here and do not
get: what did they actually write.

**Sharing.** "On", apparently by default. My record was public before I
decided it should be.

"Always" is not a toggle, it is a statement of fact in the column where a
switch would be. My client list is published permanently to the open web
and the only control is turning the entire page off.

The grants expiring on their own is genuinely good and I have never seen
a platform do it. And the line about ending a grant not pulling back what
was read is also good, and also a sentence telling me my data has already
left. Three unnamed people read my client list this week and I cannot
know who.

**ShareLink.** Written by someone who is not trying to trick me, and the
expiry is a real control. Also unusable for me. A prospective client in
Copenhagen asks for references, and sending this sends them my
competitor's name, my agency relationships, the dates I was underbooked
in 2021, and the engagement that ended in a lawyer's letter. "Send it to
someone deciding about you" describes exactly the person I least want
holding a complete map of my client base.

**Grant.** The best writing in the product. It refuses to launder a
signature into an endorsement, twice, in the reader's own view, where it
costs them something to say it. The per-chapter provenance lines are
clean and the closing note is exactly right.

Now read it as my record: forty rows, most saying no attesting party, and
a reader scrolling for a minute. Also the chapters run newest first here
and oldest first on my own screen, which will confuse anyone comparing.

**Settings.** Fine, and I like knowing the append-only fact. "Ask us"
where a list should be is worse than what I just saw on Sharing, which
already showed me a list. And nothing here lets me turn the public page
off. That is the one control I came looking for and I did not find it in
two passes.

**Public.** The one-line summary of evidence quality is decent. But this
is the leak, plainly: every client name, in order, with dates, on the
open web at a stable URL. For an employee that is a resume. For me that
is my book of business, and any competitor in Vilnius can read it over
coffee.

"Ask Liezel for the full record. It cannot be shared in part." That
button trains every reader to ask me for everything and gives me no way
to answer with less.

**PublicReduced.** They gate the pretty drawing and publish who I worked
for and when. That is exactly backwards for me. The imprint says nothing
anyone can act on. The client list is the asset.

## Adversarial

**1. The public page publishes a freelancer's client list to search
engines and the only control is all-or-nothing.** For an employee,
publishing four past employers costs nothing. For me the same field is a
competitor's prospect list, sorted by recency, with a note about when
each engagement ended so they know who is free to poach. Grain has chosen
to gate the decorative object and publish the commercially sensitive one.

**2. There is no unit for an engagement I cannot name, so nine of my
forty chapters cannot exist at all.** Two NDA clients cannot be named.
Four agency engagements have an end client who does not know my name.
Three companies are dissolved. The record has one way to represent these:
leave them off and look thinner than I am, or enter them and let them sit
permanently as unconfirmed, which looks like padding. There is no
"confirmed to exist, client withheld" state, and a system whose premise
is graded evidence has no grade for the most common condition in
independent work.

**3. Forty overlapping chapters break the list, and the design has no
overlap primitive.** The only nesting that exists is inside one business.
My spring 2023 had six clients at once. Rendered here that is six
unrelated rows with tangled dates and no grouping, and a reader scans it
as instability. The screen that is supposed to stop flattening me into a
list of job titles is a list of job titles with a longer scroll.

**4. Asking the hostile client to confirm is a permanent, unilateral,
unremovable risk with no upside path.** Grain declines to adjudicate and
I have no removal short of destroying the whole record. So I never send
that request, and the two-year retainer that produced my best work stays
unconfirmed forever, indistinguishable from a fabricated one. The record
is systematically thinnest where the stakes were highest.

**5. Four of the seven measures are structurally zero for a solo
practitioner, and nothing says so.** Four permanent low lobes on every
chapter I ever collect, encoded as a shape at the top of everything. A
reader has no reason not to read a dented figure as a small person, and
the admission that the anchors are untested makes it worse, not better,
because the uncalibrated instrument is the one that travels.

**6. The effort ledger does not close.** Forty confirmation requests, of
which three are dissolved, four are agencies with no incentive, two are
NDA, one is hostile, and the rest are people I would rather ask for
referrals than paperwork. Realistically fifteen. Then, months later, I
still cannot send a partial record, so the artifact I bought with that
effort is one I will not use. My portfolio site does the selling today at
zero marginal cost.

**7. Public and Grant train every reader to demand the whole record.**
Grain has told the reader, on the reader's screen, that partial
disclosure is impossible. So the polite request becomes everything, and
my refusal reads as concealment rather than as normal commercial
confidentiality.

**8. Read counts are visible enough to unnerve me and not specific enough
to act on.** Three unidentified reads of my client list, surfaced as a
number, with the follow-up routed to support. And the same question has
two different depths on two screens.

**9. The public page appears to be on by default and I cannot find the
switch.** The single most privacy-consequential setting is stated as a
fact rather than offered as a choice, while less consequential things get
explicit rows.

**10. Consent front-loads seven irreversible rules and asks for one
undifferentiated yes.** By the time I reach Expanded and learn the
measures are untested, I have already agreed to them.

**11. Grant reverses the chronological order used on Main.** Same label,
same five items, opposite order. At forty, walking a client through the
record on a call, we will be looking at different rows and each assume
the other is confused.

**12. The careful sentence is on the reader's screens and the collapsed
one is on mine,** which is backwards, since I know what it means and the
reader does not.

## The three things that would decide it

1. **The public page publishes my client list with no per-chapter
   control.** Gate the client names the way the imprint already is, and
   let a chapter be public, private, or named only to grant holders, so
   the record can be proved without the book of business being published.

2. **A chapter must be one named business, so my NDA work, my agency work
   and my dead clients cannot be represented at all.** Add a state where
   the engagement's existence and dates are attested by a third party
   while the client name is withheld, and grade it as its own thing
   rather than as unverified self-entry.

3. **Sharing is all-or-nothing in both directions.** Let me issue a link
   scoped to a set of chapters, with the packet stating plainly how many
   were withheld, which preserves the honesty the product is built on
   without forcing a choice between total disclosure and nothing.
