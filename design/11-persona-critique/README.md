# Worker-app persona critique, 2026-08-20

Four personas argued every worker-facing screen from rendered images of
the screens themselves, walkthrough first and adversarial second. The
findings are at [`05-findings.md`](05-findings.md), grouped by what a
reader hits rather than by who found it. The four raw critiques are the
files beside it.

Decision 053 records this directory. Decisions 054 to 059 are the founder
rulings that came out of it.

## What this is, and what it cannot support

It is synthetic. No person was interviewed. It finds comprehension
failures, missing affordances, and places where the product's epistemics
collide with what a reader expects, because those are visible in the
artifact and need no respondent.

It supports no claim about adoption, willingness to share, emotional
response, or frequency. No number in it is a measurement. Where four
personas converge, that is evidence a surface reads a certain way. It is
not evidence a market behaves a certain way, and it must not be cited as
if it were.

It binds nothing, on the same footing as [`research/`](../../research/).
A finding is a reason to look. Where a finding changed something, the
change is in `decisions/LOG.md` and the finding is cited as what prompted
it, never as what authorised it.

## Method

Each persona read PNGs of thirteen screens in first-encounter order:
consent, handle, empty, imported, the record, the expanded imprint, an
attestation landing, sharing, the outbound link, a grant as the recipient
sees it, account, the signed-in public page, and the logged-out page.
Every screen was supplied twice, as the frame that fits an iPhone 17
without scrolling and as the whole scroll flattened out, because what
fits above the fold was repeatedly the finding.

No persona saw source, templates, or design documents. Each was given the
product as a normal person would have it described to them, a history and
a set of constraints, and an instruction that every finding must name a
screen and quote or describe the element it is about.

The screens were rendered by running each artboard's own logic and
expanding its bindings, then captured headless. The canvas bundle paints
lazily and screenshots white, and a bare artboard has no runtime, so
neither was usable as evidence.

## The personas

Chosen to span the cohorts the record layer claims to serve, not to
represent a market.

- [`01-marisol-reyes.md`](01-marisol-reyes.md). 31, Bakersfield. Eight
  years across poultry processing, a food line, cash weekend work at a
  cousin's stall, warehousing, now a shift supervisor. No degree, no
  resume, prepaid data. Left one job in a wage dispute.
- [`02-devon-pratt.md`](02-devon-pratt.md). 23, a communications degree
  and a bootcamp, 180 applications and four interviews. His applied skill
  is impression management, and this product appears to take it away.
- [`03-aiste-kazlauskas.md`](03-aiste-kazlauskas.md). 38, eleven years
  independent across roughly forty clients. Overlapping engagements, two
  under NDA, three dead companies, one payment dispute that went to a
  lawyer.
- [`04-samuel-okoye.md`](04-samuel-okoye.md). 41, fourteen years of civil
  engineering in Lagos, two years in Toronto doing work far below his
  level. Nobody in Canada can check any of it. Permanent resident, and
  careful about what he puts online.
