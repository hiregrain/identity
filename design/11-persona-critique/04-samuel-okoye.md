# Samuel Okoye, 41, Toronto

Civil engineer. Fourteen years in Lagos: six with a large construction
firm running site teams on road and drainage projects, four with a state
infrastructure agency, four with an engineering consultancy. Moved to
Toronto two years ago on a skilled worker visa with his wife and two
children. Since then: eighteen months of quality inspection on a
residential build, and before that four months driving for a delivery
platform while his credentials were assessed.

Nobody in Canada can check anything about his Lagos work. Former
employers do not answer emails from Canadian numbers, one of the three no
longer operates, and the state agency has no functioning HR department.
Every Canadian employer treats fourteen years of real projects as
unverifiable, which in practice means it counts as zero. He is a
permanent resident, not yet a citizen, and deliberate about what he puts
online, because he has watched it be used against him.

Evidence tier: synthetic. Binds nothing. See [`README.md`](README.md).

## Walkthrough

**Consent.** I appreciate being told the rules first. Then: "There is no
way to show them part of it." That stops me. My whole record includes
four months of delivery driving. If I cannot show a Canadian firm the
fourteen years without also showing them that, this product is doing what
my resume already does, except now permanently and signed.

"What a reader keeps, they keep" is honest, and it is the sentence that
makes me careful for the rest of the flow. I tap agree, but I am now
reading everything.

**Handle.** My full legal name, in a URL, indexable. I have spent two
years deciding case by case which forms get my full name and which get
Sam. The screen shows the derived one and a button to claim it.

The reassurance underneath tells me the opposite of what I want to hear.
I want to know I can make an address stop working. This says I cannot.
Anything I claim today is reachable forever. I would want to change the
handle before claiming and I do not see the control.

**Empty.** "Drawn plain" is the whole product for me, and it is a bad
sentence. Every one of my Lagos chapters will sit in the plain state
permanently, because the firm will not answer, the agency has no HR, and
the third company no longer exists. This screen tells me before I have
typed anything that my career will be rendered as the empty version of
itself.

**Imported.** "None of it counts until it is." That is the sentence a
Canadian recruiter has been saying to me for two years, now printed
inside the tool that is supposed to fix it. And the button is a one-way
door. Commit to what? If I commit, does the plain dashed version of my
Lagos work become permanent and public?

There is nothing here about what happens if nobody will ever sign. No
alternative path, no way to attach a project certificate, a contract, a
photo of a signed drawing, a stamped completion letter. I have physical
documents for most of these projects. This screen has no idea they exist.
This is where I put the phone down and think for a day.

**Main.** My nationality is the second line of my own home screen and, as
I find out later, of my public page. Nobody asked me whether I wanted
country on there and I see no control.

The rest is legible and I will say it is good. The work history says
exactly who signed what. And there is one mechanism here that could work
for me: a coworker can sign. My old site engineers are on WhatsApp. Two
of them are in Calgary now.

But the record grades that lower than a business signature, visibly, and
the closing line says deleting everything is the only way out.

**Expanded.** The best screen in the app and the one that condemns me.
These are the right seven things. If someone had scored my drainage work
on consequence and on directing others, the answer would be twenty-six
men and a road that floods or does not. Those measures are exactly what
Canadian employers refuse to believe about me.

And they are only reachable through an attesting business. The section
header is literally what a named business attested. My fourteen years
produce no lobes on this figure at all. The one Canadian chapter that
will produce lobes is quality inspection, which comes out low on every
measure, because as an inspector I decided nothing and directed nobody. I
will have a filled figure that says I am small and empty space where I
was large.

**Landing.** Fine as a moment. What it teaches me is that the ring
filling in is the reward, which makes the permanently unfilled Lagos
portion feel like failure rather than distance.

**Sharing.** On by default, apparently. What is always public is
precisely the ledger of who did not confirm me. My public page
permanently broadcasts five Nigerian company names, the dates, and
"nobody has confirmed this" against most of them. That is worse than
having no page.

Credited once: the grant list with its dates, the read list, and the
honest line that ending a grant cannot pull back what a business already
kept. I trust the app more for that sentence than for anything else in
it.

**ShareLink.** "The whole history, not a selection" is the problem stated
by the product itself. A Canadian hiring manager opening this gets, in
one scroll, my four months of delivery driving, my inspection job, and
eleven years of Nigerian employers marked unconfirmed. There is no
version of this link that is my engineering career.

**Grant.** The screen I judge the product on. On my record, "no attesting
party" appears against six years of road and drainage delivery, four
years of state infrastructure, and four years of consultancy. A Canadian
reader scans top to bottom and sees confirmed Canadian low-level work and
an unconfirmed Nigerian block. The closing line is scrupulously fair and
does me no good at all, because the reader is not weighing epistemics,
they are pattern matching.

**Settings.** Mostly fine and unusually honest. Two things: the phone is
masked but the country code is not, so my phone country is another
nationality signal in the account. And who has read my record routes to
support next to a screen that already lists readers with dates.

**Public.** That summary sentence, on my record, becomes something like
"six chapters, one signed by the employer, five Samuel added himself".
That is a public, permanent, indexable summary of my unverifiability,
written in Grain's voice rather than mine, at an address that is my legal
name. This is the screen that makes me delete the account.

**PublicReduced.** The figure is gated and everything else is not. A
stranger who searches my name gets my employer list, my dates, my
country, and a per-line verdict on how much of it is unbelieved. I do not
need a Grain account to be judged by Grain.

## Adversarial

**1. The product's central mechanism is unavailable to the people whose
problem it claims to solve, and it renders that unavailability as a
permanent public verdict.** Every path to a filled figure runs through an
attesting party. My three Lagos employers cannot or will not sign, and
one no longer exists. The design has no state for "this work is real and
unconfirmable". It has one state, shown to employers, to the public and
to search engines. A resume at least lets a reader assume. Grain replaces
assumption with a signed statement of absence.

**2. The inversion: the confirmable work is the low work, so verification
correlates with how far I have fallen.** The solid lines will be delivery
driving and residential inspection; the dashed lines will be everything I
was hired to be. The measures are computed only from what got attested,
so my lobes come from the inspection job. The ring does not depict my
career, it depicts my immigration. A Canadian reader sees a confirmed
junior with a large unverified foreign claim, which is the reading I
already get for free, now cryptographically endorsed.

**3. Nationality is a fixed, unremovable field directly under my name on
every reader-facing surface,** and it has no row in the always-and-never
table, so the field with the most discrimination risk has no toggle. The
unmasked phone country code reinforces it. I have watched country of
origin do work in a hiring decision that nobody would write down. Grain
prints it above the fold, forever, at an address that is my legal name.

**4. All-or-nothing sharing is the resume's worst property, made
irreversible.** A person applying for a bridge engineering role must
transmit four months of delivery driving, not because the reader needs it
but because the integrity model requires it. The stated reason is real,
and the cost is borne entirely by people with survival work in their
history, which is disproportionately immigrants. A senior Canadian
engineer with an unbroken career pays nothing. I pay everything.

**5. Public exposure is on by default and the copy about it is
unfalsifiably reassuring.** A page I did not opt into, at a URL derived
from my legal name, that cannot be retired, listing my Nigerian
employment and how little of it is believed. That is a searchable
artifact an adversary, an employer, or an immigration process could use,
and I created it by tapping a button on a screen that framed permanence
as a benefit.

**6. The coworker path exists and the design immediately devalues it.**
The word "only", the phrase "not the business", and the visual demotion
all tell the reader that a human being physically present is a lesser
class of evidence than an HR system. For my Lagos work a coworker is the
only witness that will ever exist, and several are now senior engineers
in Canada with more local credibility than the defunct firm ever had. The
mechanism that could save this product for me is the one the copy quietly
disparages.

**7. Commit is a one-way door presented with no consequence preview.**
Nowhere on that screen does it say that committing an unconfirmable
chapter creates a permanent public line. The consequence was disclosed
three minutes earlier, in different vocabulary, before I had any
chapters. That is a consent failure, not a copy nitpick.

**8. The measures are undertested and the product says so where nobody
negotiating a job will read it.** The admission is at the bottom of a
modal. The employer's screen shows the same measures as bar figures with
no such caveat. Bars look like scores. A reader will treat them as
scores.

**9. Attestation is one directional and I cannot compel or correct it.**
An employer who ended badly writes my chapter, it is permanent, my only
remedy is an annotation, and my only exit is deleting everything. For a
migrant worker in a dispute with an employer who controls a permit, that
is not a neutral property.

**10. Deletion is a request, not a right I exercise,** and it is partial
by construction, since what a reader kept, they keep. There is no visible
timeline and no way to see what was retained, while strangers are reading
and I am given a count.

**11. Two identity signals leak past the sharing controls.** The
identity line can be paired with the country line to infer a foreign
document, and the language setting plus the phone country code make the
account itself a location record. None appear in the always-and-never
table, so my mental model of what I control is wrong.

**12. A record with one Canadian chapter reads worse in Grain than on
paper.** On paper I write fourteen years of projects and a reader decides
how much to believe. In Grain the list is sorted so that the decline is
the shape of the list, and the summary does the arithmetic out loud. Mine
reads as a ratio nobody would ever have computed about me before this
product existed.

## The three things that would decide it

1. **Unconfirmable work has exactly one representation, shown to
   employers, to visitors and to search engines,** which converts fourteen
   years of real engineering into a signed public statement of doubt. Give
   it a second evidence path a person can actually walk: named individual
   witnesses who were present, and worker-supplied documents such as
   contracts, stamped drawings and completion certificates, each with its
   own provenance and its own weaker claim.

2. **All-or-nothing sharing forces me to transmit four months of delivery
   driving to reach a bridge engineering role.** Let a sender scope by
   date range or chapter while the recipient always sees the count and
   dates of what was withheld, so the reader knows a selection was made
   and how large, and lying is still impossible.

3. **Nationality sits under my name on every reader-facing surface with
   no row in the always-and-never table.** Drop country from every
   rendered surface and keep it as an internal fact about which document
   was checked, since it says nothing about the work and everything about
   how a reader will treat me.
