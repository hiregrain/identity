# Findings

Grouped by what a reader hits, not by who found it. Each finding names
the screens it lives on and carries its disposition: what was decided,
where, or why it was accepted rather than fixed.

Evidence tier. This is synthetic critique and it binds nothing. See
[`README.md`](README.md) for what the method can and cannot support.

## The spine

**The design is scrupulously honest, and the cost of that honesty falls
entirely on the worker, disproportionately on the workers the record
layer exists for.**

A salaried worker with an unbroken history at registered employers pays
nothing for all-or-nothing sharing, for permanent unconfirmed marks, or
for a public evidence-quality summary. Marisol pays with a wage dispute
she cannot separate from eight years of work. Aiste pays with her client
list published to competitors. Samuel pays with fourteen years rendered
as a signed public statement of doubt. Devon pays least, and he is the
one who found the exploit.

Three of the four sharpest findings below are that one fact seen from
different angles. The constraint that outranks convenience requires that
marks be deterministic and that no surface collapse a graded claim into a
yes. It does not require that absence of evidence be rendered as a
statement about a person. Several of these findings were the second thing
wearing the first thing's clothes.

## The record marked the worker's honesty as doubt

`Main`, `Public`, `PublicReduced`, `Grant`.

A party's assertion read "Signed by Sunrise Foods". The worker's
identical-strength assertion read "Liezel added this. Nobody has
confirmed it" and "Processing operator, as Liezel gave it". No surface
anywhere marked a party's uncorroborated claim as unverified.

Three personas read the page as taking the employer's side. Marisol: the
page "has a phrase for my claims and a different phrase for their claims,
and mine is the weaker one every time". The marks are not randomly
distributed. They fall on people whose employers will not sign, which is
informal work, dissolved firms and cross-border history, which is the
population being sold on this.

Decided in 055. Both sides state provenance and neither states doubt. "As
X gave it" is deleted everywhere. Provenance moves to the marks, with the
sentence retained in the DOM as the mark's accessible name.

## A page-level badge was proposed and refused

Considered during the review, refused in 055. "Grain verified" is the
collapse of a graded claim into a yes, in two words, on the most widely
seen surface. It is the credential logic entry 027 exists to refuse. The
row is the mark, and "Attested by Sunrise Foods" is more informative than
"verified" at the same glance.

## The measures rendered as a score

`Expanded`, `Grant`.

Seven measures, each drawn as a partially filled ladder. All four
personas said score, unprompted, including the industrial designer.
Marisol: "Bars are a score. I know a score when I see one." Removing the
ordinal number in 042 did not remove the ordinal.

Two aggravating facts came from the artifact itself. `Expanded` admits at
the bottom that the anchors "have not been tested for whether two
managers reading the same work pick the same one", and `Grant` shows the
reader the same ladders with no such caveat. And the standing paragraph,
repeated verbatim seven times, contains the most gameable sentence in the
product: the record keeps the highest level at least two parties assert
and never an average, which Devon read correctly as an instruction for
stacking two friendly signers against one accurate one.

Decided in 054. Nothing derived from the measures renders in v1. They are
still collected. The imprint draws a constant neutral profile, which is
the only treatment that keeps the engraving, because lobe depth is level
and switching levels off collapses the figure to plain concentric rings.

## The crawlable page gated the wrong object

`PublicReduced`, `Sharing`.

The logged-out page withheld the imprint and published every party name,
every date and a per-line verdict. For Devon the flattering element was
gated and the thin one indexed. For Aiste the gated object was decorative
and the published one was her book of business, "sorted by recency, with
a note about when each engagement ended so they know who is free to
poach". For Samuel it was a permanent indexed statement of what nobody
had confirmed, at a URL that is his legal name.

Partly decided, partly accepted. 056 sets the page off by default,
carries employer, position and dates with provenance by mark, and puts
the imprint in front of human visitors but never in the crawler payload,
because the irrevocability argument survives even though the sensitivity
argument does not. The client-list exposure is an accepted cost recorded
in 053: a worker who publishes a record publishes who they worked for.

## Country of origin sat under the name on every surface

`Main`, `Grant`, `Public`, `PublicReduced`, and absent from Sharing's
always-and-never table.

Samuel: the field with the most discrimination risk on the page had no
disclosure and no control, and Settings reinforced it with an unmasked
phone country code. Decided in 055: it comes off every rendered surface.

## The worker saw a weaker qualification than the reader

`Main` against `Grant`, `Public`, `PublicReduced`.

`Main` said "Identity verified". Every reader-facing surface said
"Identity document checked, which says nothing about the work below". The
careful sentence was where it costs Grain something to say it; the badge
was on the surface a worker screenshots. The same inversion appeared with
the calibration caveat, which only the worker could see.

Decided in 055, with the general rule stated: the worker never sees a
weaker qualification than the reader does.

## Omission was free while inclusion was permanent

`Imported`, `Main`, `Public`, `Grant`.

Devon and Marisol independently derived the same play: import the resume,
commit only the chapters that will attest, leave the rest off. Adding is
permanent; never adding costs nothing. No surface showed that a selection
had occurred, so a reader could not distinguish asked and refused from
never asked from never entered.

Decided in 059. The packet states that a record contains only the
chapters the worker entered. The record does not compute unaccounted
time, because gap detection converts unexplained months into a fresh
accusation computed by Grain about a person, which is the logic being
refused.

## The consent instrument front-loaded terms nobody had reached

`Consent`.

Seven blocks before the reader had typed anything, with the button
already tappable at the bottom. Four of the seven described consequences
of actions not yet taken. Devon skipped to the button. Marisol stopped on
"It is permanent, and you cannot edit it" before she had entered a name.
Aiste had decided she was evaluating rather than adopting by the third
block.

Two things were buried inside paragraphs whose headings promised
something else: that readers "can pay us to analyse what they read", and
that the seven measures travel onto the public page.

Decided in 058. Only what governs having a record at all is said at
signup; every other term is said at the moment of its own action, with a
flag recorded there. Whether the instrument can be split without
weakening consent is a question for `counsel/`, not an assumption.

## The highest-effort screen returned a zero

`Imported`.

The fixed bar reads "none of it counts until it is" above a fully dotted
ring, a heading of "5 chapters, none of them confirmed", and a button
labelled "Commit these 5 chapters". Marisol: "I just gave you my whole
working life and you told me it does not count." Every persona hesitated
here and two put the phone down.

Samuel identified it as a consent failure rather than a copy problem:
nothing on the screen says that committing an unconfirmable chapter
creates a permanent public line, and that consequence was disclosed
minutes earlier in different vocabulary before he had any chapters.

Partly decided. 058 renames the action, since the irreversible step is
asking a party to attest and not recording your own account. The framing
of `Empty` and `Imported` toward one chapter rather than a career is
execution work under 053, and a second import path is flagged as scope
rather than proposed.

## The mechanism that rescues the hardest cases was disparaged

`Main`, `Public`, `Grant`.

"Only a coworker has signed this". "Signed by a coworker, not the
business". Samuel: for Lagos work a named coworker is the only witness
that will ever exist, and several of his are now senior engineers in
Canada with more local credibility than the dissolved firm ever had. The
copy told the reader that a human present at the work is a lesser class
of evidence than an HR system.

Decided in 055, copy changed. The wider gap, worker-supplied documents
such as contracts, stamped drawings and completion certificates, is a
gate in `plans/ORDER.md` and not designed here, because accepting a
document means Grain asserting something about it.

## The share link handed the record to whoever held it

`ShareLink`, `Sharing`, `Grant`.

Reads could not be attributed, which is why Sharing could only report "3
this week". Revocation was per link, not per reader. The loudest copy in
the product was the warning that the link cannot be un-sent.

Decided in 057. The link becomes a grant addressed to a named person who
opens it by proving control of the address it was sent to. Reads are
identified, revocation is per party, and the hazard the warning described
stops existing. An account is required for analytics and for depth, never
to read a record.

## The account surface stated a number nobody could open

`Settings`.

"47 entries, and none removed" with no list. Marisol read it as
surveillance: five jobs against forty-seven entries written by somebody
else. Separately, "Who has read my record: Ask us" routed to support what
`Sharing` already answered two taps away, and every account change was a
support request.

Decided in 059. The count is demoted and opens the ledger, which is also
where the consent flags from 058 become visible.

## Two smaller defects, straight to execution

Chapter ordering ran oldest-first on `Main` and `Public` and newest-first
on `PublicReduced` and `Grant`. Aiste found it and it is confirmed in
source: same label, same five chapters, opposite order.

`Sharing` demonstrated a former employer, Sunrise Foods Manufacturing,
holding a live grant to the worker's current record. Two personas read it
as "my old boss is watching me now". Whatever the mechanism permits, the
demonstration data teaches the wrong thing.

## Accepted costs

Recorded in 053 so they are not rediscovered as defects.

All-or-nothing content sharing stands. The worker controls who reads,
never what they read. The disputed employer rides along on every grant,
the freelancer cannot withhold an NDA client, and the engineer transmits
four months of delivery driving to reach an engineering role. The cost is
real, it falls unevenly, and it is accepted.

Party names are public on a record the worker chooses to publish.

There is no reading path for someone who will not click a link in their
own email, and no account-driven growth from record reads.

## What the personas defended

Named here because it would be easy to trade away while fixing the
findings above.

`Grant` was praised by every persona, including the two most hostile to
the product. "Grain checks that a business really signed a record. It
never checks whether the work was done well." Aiste: "That is the best
writing in the product. It refuses to launder a signature into an
endorsement, twice, in the reader's own view, where it costs them
something to say it."

Also defended: grants that expire on their own date; the statement that
ending a grant cannot pull back what a reader already kept; "Your phone
and email, never in a link"; the handle guarantee that an old address
keeps working and never goes to anyone else; the append-only reading on
`Settings`; and the imprint as an object, which all four called the best
thing in the product and none of them could read.
